MiniLab 3 render check

Every preset plays the same phrase offline (a held low note, a held chord, then 8 fast short notes) and the result is checked: no NaN, not silent, no clicks at note starts and ends, no sound stopping dead, no level far from the other presets. The last columns compare each render with the saved reference: loudness, loudness over time, and the chord's spectrum, in dB. Loudness over time and the spectrum are measured on the sound in mono, (left + right) / 2, so a change in stereo width alone shows there as 0.0. Random parts of the sound (noise, unison phases, the random source, the random LFO wave) are seeded, so every render of a preset is exactly the same until the engine changes.
