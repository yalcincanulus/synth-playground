MiniLab 3 render check

Every preset plays the same phrase offline (a held low note, a held chord, then 8 fast short notes) and the result is checked: no NaN, not silent, no clicks at note starts and ends, no sound stopping dead, no level far from the other presets. The last columns compare each render with the saved reference: loudness, loudness over time, and the chord's spectrum, in dB. Random parts of the sound (noise, unison phases, the random source) are seeded, so the same engine gives the same result every time; only the random LFO wave can still vary a little.
